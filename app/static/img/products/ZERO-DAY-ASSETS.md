# ZERO-DAY frontend assets

- `zero-day-bag.glb`: geometry from Coffee Packaging by walteraraujo,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/coffee-packaging-62e5f2ddf6f1437db4da89791a3200eb
  licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
  The embedded third-party texture, its image bufferView, textures and samplers
  were removed; the geometry and original attribution remain. The application
  supplies a matte material and a ZERO-DAY label before displaying any frame.
  The original `coffee_packaging.glb` is preserved. Public credit is in the footer.
- `cereza-editorial.webp` and `bergamota-editorial.webp`: resized WebP copies of
  the user-supplied PNGs (1000px, quality 82). Originals are preserved.
- `../editorial/ritual-plantacion.webp`: resized copy of the user-supplied
  cafeplantacion.jpg (1600px, quality 80). Photo grading is applied in CSS.

No Costa, Dunkin, espresso-machine or cappuccino model is loaded by this page.
